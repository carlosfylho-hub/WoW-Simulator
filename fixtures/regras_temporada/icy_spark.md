# Spark Crafting Guide for Midnight

Two sparks are needed to create most crafted gear, with four being required for
gear that fills two slots at once, specifically two-handed weapons.

By default, epic items created with the Spark can go up to 305 item level, but
you can add 80 Hero or Myth crests as optional reagents in order to make them go
up to 318 or 331 item level at maximum quality!

| Crest | Main Sources | Item Level Range |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No Crest | Weekly Expansion Hub Quests | 292 to 305 |
| Hero | Heroic Raids, M+ from 4 to 8, Tier 11 Delves | 305 to 318 |
| Myth | Mythic Raids, M+ 9 or higher | 318 to 331 |
