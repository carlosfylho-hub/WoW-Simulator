# How to Unlock Ascendant Venomstones in WoW Midnight Season 2: Neck, Weapon & Trinket Upgrades

WoW Midnight Patch 12.1.5 introduces Ascendant Venomstones, these work the same way as the Ascendant Voidcores in Midnight Season 1; you will be able to use the Ascendant Venomstones to further upgrade your max upgraded Hero and Myth Track level Weapons, Crafted Weapons, Trinkets and now Necklaces to an even higher item level than before.

In order to upgrade any of your items you will need to collect 10 Ascendant Venomstones.

## Ascendant Venomstone Item Level Upgrades

Once you are able to start collecting Ascendant Venomstones, you can use 10 of them to increase the item level of fully upgraded Hero and Myth Track Weapons, Crafted Weapons, Trinkets and Necklaces.

| **Item Level Track** | **Max Item Level Pre-Ascended** | **Max Item Level After Ascended** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Myth Track Items | 334 | 341 |
| Hero Track Items | 321 | 328 |
| Tidal Crafted Myth Weapon | 331 | 337 |
| Tidal Crafted Hero Weapon | 318 | 325 |

After using an Ascendant Venomstone on an item, the item will become Ascended and will no longer be able to receive any more upgrades.
